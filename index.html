02 — Brand Overview

We don't document
spaces. We reveal them.

New Found Visuals is the creative arm of LVN Studio — a real estate photography and videography brand built for agents, developers, and luxury listings who refuse to settle for ordinary.

Founded by Diego NFV approaches every property as a creative brief, not a listing. The result is imagery that earns attention, commands premium positioning, and reflects the ambition of the people who sell it.

This system is designed to be applied consistently across all touchpoints — from social content and websites to printed collateral and client presentations.

Two voices. One editorial, one structural. The serif carries emotion and presence — the sans-serif delivers clarity and order. Together they define a hierarchy that feels curated, never corporate.

Logo
System

The NFV wordmark derives its authority from spacing and weight — not decoration. The mark should always command quiet attention, never compete with imagery. Restraint is the design.

Wordmark — Longform

Clear Space

Maintain minimum clear space of 1× the cap-height of "NFV" on all sides. Never crowd the mark with text or imagery. It needs room to breathe — that's part of the brand.

Minimum Size

The wordmark "NFV" should never appear smaller than 24px on screen or 6mm in print. The full "New Found Visuals" lockup should never appear below 100px wide digital.

Approved Backgrounds

Off White, Soft Black, and Sand only. Never place the logo on busy photographic backgrounds without a clean overlay field or isolation zone first.

What Not To Do

Do not stretch or distort · Do not add drop shadows · Do not outline or stroke · Do not use unauthorized colors · Do not rotate or tilt.

07 — Visual Direction

Visual
Identity

NFV images should look like they belong in a magazine before they belong on a listing. Every frame is composed. Every shadow considered. Think negative space, natural gradients, and rooms that feel like they have a mood.

Primary Editorial Frame
Detail Composition
Exterior Architecture
Interior — Wide
Lifestyle Context

Architectural Framing

Use the architecture itself as the compositional framework. Lines lead. Geometry balances. Rooms are framed like structures, not snapshots.

Intentional Light

Natural window light, dramatic contrast, or soft ambient fills. Never flash-on-lens. Light tells the story of the time of day.

Editorial Cropping

Tight on the interesting. Loose where the space earns it. Strategic cropping creates curiosity and tension — not every room needs to be shown in full.

Tonal Restraint

Editing should feel like a decision, not a filter. Muted shadows, controlled highlights, warm neutrals or cool monochromes — never over-saturated.

Avoid
HDR / Tone-mapped Fish-eye Lens On-camera Flash Over-saturated Edits Generic MLS Angles Cluttered Props Low-quality Virtual Staging Barrel Distortion

08 — Tone of Voice

Voice &
Tone

NFV speaks like a creative director, not a sales representative. Assured without being arrogant. Minimal without being cold. It has a perspective — and it's not afraid to show it.

Every property has something worth seeing.
We find the frame that proves it.

Confident

We don't hedge. Short sentences, direct phrasing. If the work is excellent, we say so — without qualification or apology.

Minimal

Every word earns its place. If a sentence can be shorter, it should be. White space in writing works the same as in design.

Slightly Artistic

We reference craft, light, composition, architecture. This isn't showing off — it's signaling to the right client that we speak their language.

Not Salesy

Never "Book now and get 20% off!" — instead, "Ready when you are." The brand assumes quality. It doesn't beg for business.

Write Like This

Caption: Architecture doesn't wait for perfect light. We do.

Bio: Real estate photography and video for spaces that mean something. Based in [City], shooting nationally.

CTA: View the work. Then let's talk.

Not Like This

Avoid: "We provide HIGH QUALITY real estate photography at AFFORDABLE prices for agents!"

Avoid: "Our passionate team goes above and beyond to deliver stunning visuals that make your listings POP!!!"

Avoid: "Contact us today for a FREE quote — limited time offer!"
