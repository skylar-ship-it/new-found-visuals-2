Good to Know

Policies

Cancellations & Rescheduling
Requests within 48 hours may incur a fee of $100+ depending on scheduling impact. Weather-related cancellations are exempt.
Property Readiness
The property must be fully staged and ready upon arrival. Delays from cleaning, restricted access, or unpreparedness may result in extra fees or reduced coverage.
Revisions
Minor revisions are included. Major creative direction changes or multiple revision rounds beyond standard scope may require additional fees.
Delivery Approval
Final delivery is considered approved if feedback isn't received within ~72 hours of delivery.
Usage Rights
Pricing covers standard organic use — social media, MLS, and personal websites. Paid advertising or extended commercial usage may require additional licensing.
Drone & Exterior
Drone and exterior footage are subject to weather conditions, site access, and local FAA regulations.
