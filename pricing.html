Policies
Cancellations & Rescheduling
Requests within 48 hours may be subject to a $100+ fee based on scheduling impact. Weather-related cancellations are exempt.
Property Readiness
The property must be fully prepared upon arrival. Delays from cleaning, staging, or restricted access may result in additional fees or reduced coverage.
Delivery & Revisions
Final delivery is approved unless feedback is provided within ~72 hours. Major restructuring or multiple revision rounds beyond minor edits may incur additional fees.
Usage Rights
Pricing covers standard organic usage across social media, MLS, and personal websites. Paid advertising or extended commercial use may require additional licensing.
Drone & Exterior
Exterior and drone footage are subject to weather conditions, site access, and local FAA regulations. Rescheduling for weather is always complimentary.
Turnaround
Standard turnaround is 2–5 business days for video. Rush delivery is available upon request — additional fee may apply based on timeline.
